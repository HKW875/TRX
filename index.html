Our Services

GrowthBase platform provides comprehensive tools and professional support to help families protect, grow and transfer their assets securely.

Asset Documentation & Secure Vault

Store all your important documents (title deeds, bank statements, wills, insurance policies) in a secure, encrypted digital vault accessible only to you and authorized next of kin.

Capital and Financial Solutions

GrowthBase provides Premium and Lifetime Plan users access to capital and financial solutions to grow themselves or their business. Solutions such as loans, equipment, consulting, among other solutions to meet your personal or business growth targets .

Succession Planning Guidance

Step-by-step guidance on obtaining Grant of Representation, appointing administrators, and handling property and financial succession.

Profile Search & Claim Support

Search for unclaimed or deceased family members' profiles and initiate secure claim requests with proper verification.

Legal & Professional Referrals

Connect with verified advocates, public trustees, and estate planners specializing in succession matters.

Family Legacy Protection

Tools to ensure your assets are discovered and properly transferred to the right beneficiaries even if you are no longer around.
